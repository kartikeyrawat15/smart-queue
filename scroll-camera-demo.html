00 — GATE

Scroll to fly the camera.

The scene is fixed. Your scroll is only a number that says how far along a 3D path the camera should be.

01 — APPROACH

One continuous path.

Waypoints are threaded onto a Catmull-Rom spline, so the camera glides — it never cuts.

02 — DRIFT

Eased, not snapped.

Each frame the camera damps toward the scroll target. That weight is the whole "premium" feel.

03 — DESCENT

Fog does the depth.

No expensive render passes — just scene fog plus CSS vignette and grain on top.

04 — THE MOON

Everything is code.

Gates, ground, moon — all built procedurally at runtime. Nothing is downloaded.

05 — AFTERLIGHT

Now open the source.

Read the comments top to bottom. The core is ~40 lines of camera logic.
